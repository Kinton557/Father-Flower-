Our story

How we began

Father Flower Educational Network, formerly Father Flower Group of Schools, was founded in 2021 by Dr Jimmy Lemekani-Waya. It offers primary and high school education, with boarding and day facilities.

From the start the network has held to one promise: affordable education for all, delivered with discipline, order and care.

Vision

To be the leading provider of world-class basic education by 2030.

Mission

Moulding a disciplined learner for national and global impact.

Our leadership philosophy

Leadership through discipline and service

Our motto speaks of military leadership styles. At Father Flower this is about character, not aggression. It means learning from the best of military tradition: punctuality, neatness, respect for others, clear responsibility, and putting the team before oneself.

Learners are guided by firm, fair and caring routines. Rules are explained, standards are the same for everyone, and every learner is given chances to lead, from class monitors to prefects. The aim is simple: young people who can lead themselves, serve others and stand tall in Zimbabwe and beyond.
