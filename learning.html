Learning

Mind, Body and Spirit

A full school life: strong academics, active sport, the arts and, where offered, boarding.

Academics

Learning with purpose

Learners follow Zimbabwe's national curriculum from primary through high school, taught by committed teachers in a calm, orderly setting.

  • Regular tests, exercises and exams
  • Clear term report cards for parents
  • Online portal for results, assignments and notices

Pathways

ZIMSEC and Cambridge

Which examinations are offered depends on the campus. Please confirm with the campus you are interested in.

ZIMSEC

The national examinations set by the Zimbabwe School Examinations Council, taken at the end of primary school and at Ordinary and Advanced Level.

Cambridge

An international pathway available where the campus offers it. Ask the school office which campuses and levels it covers.

Sport

Teamwork on and off the field

Sport builds fitness, resilience and team spirit. Learners take part in inter-house and inter-school competition.

Arts and culture

Confidence and creativity

Music, dance, drama and cultural events give every learner a stage and a sense of pride in who they are.

Boarding life

A safe home away from home

Boarding and day facilities are offered. Boarders live in supervised hostels with set routines for study, meals, rest and recreation, and parents can follow exeats and updates through the portal.

Boarding is available at selected campuses. See the Boarding badge on our schools page.

Transport

Getting to school

Transport arrangements differ from campus to campus. Please ask your campus office about routes and costs, or call the head office on +263 773 526 109.
