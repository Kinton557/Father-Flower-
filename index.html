Our story

A school built on discipline and opportunity

Founded in 2021, Father Flower Educational Network brings primary and high school education to families across Zimbabwe. We believe every child deserves a well-ordered school, committed teachers and a real chance to lead.

Our three pillars

Discipline. Order. Service.

01

Discipline

Punctuality, respect and self-control are practised daily, so learners build habits that last well beyond school.

02

Order

A clear structure for every day: routines, uniforms and expectations that help learners focus and feel safe.

03

Service

Leadership means serving others. Learners learn teamwork, responsibility and care for their community.
